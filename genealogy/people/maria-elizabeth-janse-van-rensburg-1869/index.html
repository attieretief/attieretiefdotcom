Attie’s maternal great-great-grandmother — Sophia van Niekerk’s mother. Baptised 31 October 1869 at Albanie, Cape Province; her FamilySearch profile carries 26 sources. Married Johan Wilhelm Albertus van Niekerk at Riversdal on 5 November 1889 and raised ten children with him.

Her parents Petrus Johannes Janse van Rensburg (1849–1934) and Sophia Wilhelmina Jacoba van Zyl (1852–1926) were married at Riversdal on 29 September 1868. The grandmother is the source of Sophia van Niekerk’s full given names, carried forward whole to the granddaughter.
