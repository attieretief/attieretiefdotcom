  • Josef "Jopie" Johannes Fourie du Plessis 1914–2006

Izelle’s paternal great-grandmother — Jopie du Plessis’s mother. Married Marthinus Petrus du Plessis at Jacobsdal in 1903 and raised ten children with him in the Fauresmith / Luckhoff district of the Free State. Her maiden name being Jansen van Rensburg (not Fourie) is one of the pieces of evidence that shifts Jopie’s “Fourie” middle name from a maternal-surname reading toward commemoration of the Boer martyr Jopie Fourie.

Her own Jansen van Rensburg ancestry is not separately traced on this site.

  • The Fourie middle name — mother or martyr?

    Jopie du Plessis carries a Fourie middle name. Once thought to point at a Fourie mother up the line, it now looks more like commemoration of the Boer martyr Jopie Fourie, added at baptism in the weeks after the martyr's December 1914 execution.
