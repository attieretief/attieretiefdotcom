Christened at Rudgwick, Sussex, in 1790; died at Piketberg in 1835. With his wife Sophia Pearce, the English origin of the Laker thread that entered the Retief line a generation later through their daughter Anne Laker.

Threads that mention James Laker

  • The Sussex family that landed at Piketberg

    James Laker was christened in Rudgwick, Sussex in 1790. By 1819 the family had crossed to the Cape — and specifically to Piketberg, the exact small farming district the Retiefs had been working since the early 1700s.

  • Two English settler threads, on either side

    The family runs back to two different small English villages — Rudgwick in Sussex on the paternal side, Bodham in Norfolk on the maternal. Either would be unusual for an Afrikaans family. Both is striking.
