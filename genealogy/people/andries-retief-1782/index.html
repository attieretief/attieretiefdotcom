Gen 4 of the paternal spine. Named on the 1843 Death Notice of his son (Andries Johannes Retief, 1817–1843) as the father of the deceased — so this generation is anchored by the same primary document that anchors Gen 5. His wife Sophia Maria Elizabeth Bauermeister is named in the same field.

Few independent details have been recovered for him beyond the marriage and the Death Notice naming. The line continues here.
