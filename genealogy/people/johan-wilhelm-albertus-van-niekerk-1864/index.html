Attie’s paternal great-great-grandfather on the van Niekerk line — Sophia van Niekerk’s father. Married Maria Elizabeth Janse van Rensburg at Riversdal on 5 November 1889 and raised ten children with her. His FamilySearch profile carries 29 sources.

His name — Johan Wilhelm Albertus — carries down as a namesake to his grandson, Johan Wilhelm Albertus van Zyl (b. 1920), Attie’s maternal grandfather. See the naming-echo thread.

His own parents are Johannes Wilhelmus Albertus van Niekerk (1836–1876) and Dorothea Wilhelmina Oosthuysen (1840–1870), married at Riversdale on 19 May 1856.
