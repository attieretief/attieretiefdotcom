Attie’s paternal grandmother. The 1906 DRC baptism entry at Calitzdorp anchors her and names her parents — the first rung up the Saayman line, opening a well-documented climb through the Dutch Reformed Church registers of Calitzdorp and Laingsburg.

Schooled at Laingsburg; married Adriaan Albertus Retief at Ladismith on 31 December 1930.
