  • William Doughty Frost 1813–1887

The documented English-settler progenitor of the Frost line. Born and christened at Bodham, Norfolk in February 1787; died at Port Elizabeth in January 1867 and buried at Coega. His FamilySearch profile carries 28 sources and 19 memories — heavily attested by the standards of the maternal side and the firmest anchor in this whole branch of the family.

Threads that mention Philip Frost

  • Two English settler threads, on either side

    The family runs back to two different small English villages — Rudgwick in Sussex on the paternal side, Bodham in Norfolk on the maternal. Either would be unusual for an Afrikaans family. Both is striking.

  • The 1840s Frost–Terblanche blend

    Norfolk and the Karoo, in the same household by the 1840s. William Doughty Frost married Susanna Magdalena Terblanche — English settler and Afrikaans trekboer blending early, long before it was the norm.
