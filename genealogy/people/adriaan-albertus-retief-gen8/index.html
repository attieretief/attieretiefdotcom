Adriaan Albertus Retief

civil record · The Retief Spine — François to today · Gen 8
Spouse
Maria Margaretha Saayman · m. 31 Dec 1930, Ladismith, Cape

Gen 8 of the paternal spine — Attie’s paternal grandfather. Married Maria Margaretha Saayman on 31 December 1930 at Ladismith. The first of three Adriaan Albertus Retiefs in a row (Gens 8–9–10), the name carried straight down to Attie himself.

Through his wife, the Saayman line enters the family — well-documented two generations up through the DRC church registers of Calitzdorp and Laingsburg.
