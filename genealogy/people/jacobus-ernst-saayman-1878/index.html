Jacobus Ernst Saayman

1878–1958 church register · The Saayman Line

Born and baptised at Calitzdorp in 1878; died at Laingsburg in 1958 — the geographic move that explains why his daughter Maria Margaretha was schooled at Laingsburg. His baptism register entry names his parents, Johannes Mattheus Saayman × Maria Margaretha Kotze, opening one further rung up the Saayman line.

The deeper question — whether this Saayman branch joins the Daniel Zaaijman / Krotoa root or a separate progenitor — remains open.
