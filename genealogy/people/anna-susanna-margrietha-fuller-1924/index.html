Attie’s maternal grandmother — born at Jacobsdal in the Orange Free State on 14 June, exactly fifty years before Attie himself was born on the same date. Married Johan Wilhelm Albertus van Zyl at Jacobsdal in January 1945 (civil record).

Through her, the Fuller and Frost lines — both English-settler families that entered the Cape in the 19th century — enter the family.
