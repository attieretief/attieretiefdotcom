Attie’s maternal grandfather. Born 22 February 1920. The 1942 Johannesburg driver’s licence and 1955 identity card — both supplied by the family — anchor him independently of the tree records: they match the FamilySearch profile (GLMV-MXY) exactly and place him in Johannesburg by 1942. He had married Anna Susanna Margrietha Fuller three years later at Jacobsdal in the Free State.

His name — Johan Wilhelm Albertus — is not chosen at random. It carries down the full Christian-name block of his maternal grandfather, Johan Wilhelm Albertus van Niekerk (1864–1938), across a surname change. See the naming-echo thread.
