The Meades Line

family tree

Izelle’s paternal grandmother, Francina Carolina Meades (1915–2000), sat at the wall of the daughters’ side of the family until the 1951 Koffiefontein marriage record led to her existing FamilySearch profile — which arrives with four generations already attached.

Line-up (bottom-up):

  • Francina Carolina Meades — b. 16 Dec 1915, Petrusburg; d. 15 Dec 2000. m. Jopie du Plessis, 1951, Koffiefontein. Izelle’s paternal grandmother.
  • David Jacobus Meades (1885–1960) × Maria Dorothea Petronella van Tonder (1887–1975) — Francina’s parents. Twelve children.
  • Jan Wolfaard Meades (1857–1940) × Francina Carolina Scholtz (1861–1930) — Izelle’s great-great-grandparents. Married 17 September 1883 at Dutoit’s Pan in the alluvial-diamond diggings that had grown into the town of Kimberley by then. Nine children.

The naming echo runs down the female side of this line: Francina Carolina Scholtz (1861–1930) → Francina Carolina Meades (1915–2000) — grandmother’s given names carried forward intact.

Open questions

  • Jan Wolfaard Meades’s parents. The next rung up on the surname line. What was the Meades family doing in Griqualand West by the 1880s? The Dutoit’s Pan wedding is a diamond-rush marriage — worth understanding the earlier passage into the Cape.
  • The van Tonder side. Maria Dorothea Petronella van Tonder’s own ancestry is not yet traced. A future thread.
