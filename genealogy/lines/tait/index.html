The Tait Line

family-confirmed

The richest and best-attested of the married-in lines, and it belongs to Attie and Izelle’s daughters.

Izelle’s mother, Johanna Petronella Tait (b. 1954), is the daughter of Pieter Johannes Tait (1928–2014) and Ammerentia Petronella Duvenage (1927–2013), who married on 29 December 1951 at Verkeerdevlei, Brandfort, in the Free State. Izelle herself confirmed that her grandmother was Ammerentia — so this line is not only record-matched and tree-attached but family-confirmed. The name Petronella carries straight down: grandmother Ammerentia Petronella → mother Johanna Petronella.

From Pieter Johannes the Tait surname climbs seven further generations to George Tait (1765–1851) and Joshua Tait, with the deepest rungs showing unmistakable Scottish-Borders names — Trumbull, Nesbit, Johnston, Barr. A genuine Scottish settler family that entered through the Eastern Cape (residence at Queenstown) and drifted into the Free State by the twentieth century.

Line up: Johanna Petronella Tait (1954) → Pieter Johannes Tait (1928–2014) → Isaac Willem Tait (1882–1939) → Cornelis Johannes Tait (1839–1908) → John Tait (1811–1898) → Isaac Tait (1783–1851) → George Tait (1765–1851) → Joshua Tait. Many Cape families marry in along the way: Swanepoel, Gouws, Stroh, Meyer, van der Nest, Maartens, Barnard, van Nimwegen, Crause, Botha, van Wyngaarden.

Caveat: the deep generations rest on the FamilySearch shared tree — well-structured and internally consistent, but community-sourced. The grandparental link (Pieter & Ammerentia) is family-confirmed.
