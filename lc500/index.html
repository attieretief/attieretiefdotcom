Story

The arc as it stands. Chapters with a dashed marker are hypotheses waiting on a record.

3 May 2017 · Japan

Built

The car was built on 3 May 2017, in the LC's first year of production, as a right-hand-drive South African car. Lexus built the LC at its Motomachi plant in Toyota City, so this one was very probably built there. It left the factory in Naples Yellow over black, with a "hold delivery" flag on its order.

Verified build date and spec: Lexus SA vehicle record. Probable plant: Wikipedia, Lexus LC.

June 2017 · Franschhoek

The launch car

Lexus launched the LC 500 to the South African motoring press in June 2017 at the Franschhoek Motor Museum, priced from R1 729 600, with about forty cars allocated for the year. The yellow car in the launch photographs carries Gauteng press-fleet plates. TimesLive's first drive, published 21 June 2017, was also in a Naples Yellow car.

Halfway Lexus confirmed to the buyer by phone on 17 October 2026 that this is the Lexus launch car. The record agrees. The car was built six weeks before the launch and its order carried a delivery hold. It was sold through a dealer in late August and had only 3 617 km on it a year later. That is what a car looks like after a launch and a short press loan before it goes to its first customer.

Probable Dealer confirmation, verbal, 17 Oct 2026. Documentary proof still wanted: the eNaTIS registration history, or Toyota SA's press fleet records matching the launch plate to this VIN. See Wanted. Launch coverage: Cars.co.za launch review, TimesLive first drive (21 June 2017).

24 August 2017 – 2020 · KwaZulu-Natal

First owner, Durban

Lexus Kingsmead in Durban sold the car on 24 August 2017. It came with a six-year, 100 000 km service plan. The first owner put it on a personalised KwaZulu-Natal plate and drove it gently: 3 617 km by its first service in September 2018.

The early years have one theme, the air-conditioning. It was reported dead in November 2018, not cooling in February 2019, and repaired under warranty for a leak in January 2020. The front discs were replaced in September 2019 at about 5 000 km, which is early and still unexplained.

Verified Lexus SA service history and service-plan record.

2021 – 2026 · Gauteng

Gauteng years

By November 2021 the car was on Gauteng plates, first a personalised one and then, from 2022, the number it carries today. Whether the plate changes mark one owner or two is not yet known. The last owner is a collector who keeps his cars at Halfway Lexus Pretoria. The collection includes Lamborghinis, Ferraris and several one-off Lexus models, and to its salesman the LC was the pick of them. A four-year extended warranty was bought upfront in October 2021, and every service stayed inside the Lexus network.

It still did not travel far: about 1 300 km a year. The record shows a repair after a complaint in August 2022, sound-system work in 2023, Lexus Value Services in November 2024 and November 2025, and all four rims resprayed in June 2025.

Verified plates, dates and work: Lexus SA records. Probable the last owner: dealer voice note, 17 Oct 2026. Unverified number of owners.

February – October 2026 · Pretoria

Halfway Lexus

A fresh three-year Lexus Unlimited extended warranty began on 20 February 2026. In March, Halfway Lexus Pretoria (dealer 45250) fitted Apple CarPlay, which has since been removed. In August, Halfway repaired an armrest under warranty. The car was never really for sale. Tylor asked the owner whether he could market it. The owner, who did not need the money, said he would let it go for R2 million and otherwise keep it. In October the car stood in Halfway's showroom under the "Congratulations, you've arrived" sign, advertised at R1 999 900 with 19 900 km.

According to the salesman's voice note, the car has had a full chrome delete, which explains the colour of the rims. He says the rims are unmarked, the air-con has been regassed and everything works: "the cleanest LC I have had on my floor."

Verified warranty, CarPlay and armrest jobs: Lexus SA records. Probable the sale and the cosmetic work: dealer voice notes, 17 Oct 2026, machine-transcribed from Afrikaans.

16 – 17 October 2026 · Pretoria to Cape Town

The weekend it was sold

The buyer was in Cape Town. His brief had started as a sports car under R1 million, stretched to R1.5 million, and wandered through a 911, an R8, an Aston Martin DB9 and a Vantage. On Friday evening Halfway sent showroom photos and a walk-up video. On Saturday morning they sent detail shots and the Lexus service screens.

That same morning, a salesman at a Cape Town Lexus dealer messaged Halfway. He asked them to pull the advert until Monday and offered R2 million so he could show the car to his own client. The buyer asked for an offer to purchase. Version 1 arrived at 10:54. His terms went back at 11:25, version 2 arrived at 11:46, and he had signed by 12:24, subject to finance.

Verified the correspondence below.

August 2026 · context

The end of the line

Lexus ended LC production in August 2026, with no successor announced. Nine years after it was built, the car is changing hands just as the model passes into the "they don't make them any more" category.

Probable Carscoops, January 2026, citing a Lexus dealer letter: 2026 is the last model year and production ends August 2026.
