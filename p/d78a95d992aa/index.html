Story

The arc in broad strokes. The detail is in the service record. Dashed markers are still to come.

May 2017 · Japan

Built

The car was built in the LC's first production year as a right-hand-drive South African car, in Naples Yellow over black.

Verified Lexus SA vehicle record.

June 2017 · Franschhoek

The launch car

Lexus South Africa launched the LC 500 to the press at the Franschhoek Motor Museum, with about forty cars coming to the country that year. This was the launch car.

Probable Confirmed verbally by Halfway Lexus; paperwork still wanted. Coverage: Cars.co.za, TimesLive.

2017 – 2020 · Durban

First owner

Sold new by Lexus Kingsmead in August 2017 and kept in KwaZulu-Natal. It was driven sparingly and serviced by Lexus.

Verified Lexus SA records.

2021 – 2026 · Gauteng

The collector

The car moved to Gauteng and ended up with a collector who keeps his cars at Halfway Lexus Pretoria. He kept it under extended warranty and serviced by Lexus, and still drove it only about 1 300 km a year.

Verified Lexus SA records. Probable the owner: dealer, 17 Oct 2026.

August 2026

The end of the line

Lexus South Africa had already dropped the LC from its line-up by early 2024. In August 2026 Lexus ended LC production worldwide, with no successor announced.

Probable Carscoops, citing a Lexus dealer letter.

October 2026 · Pretoria

Sold

The car was never really for sale. The salesman persuaded the owner to name a price, and it went on the floor with 19 900 km. A buyer in Cape Town who had set out to find a sports car under a million rand signed for it within a day. Another dealer was trying to secure it for his own client that same morning.

Verified dealer correspondence, 16–17 Oct 2026.

Collectability

The case for the car holding or growing its value is a stack of things that cannot be repeated. It is a discontinued model. It has the last naturally aspirated V8 Lexus made. It sold in tiny numbers in South Africa and was withdrawn here early. And this car was the launch car, in the launch colour, with low mileage and an unbroken dealer history.

The case against is the market so far. LCs have mostly depreciated like other luxury GTs; a low-mileage 2018 LC 500h sold at auction in the US in July 2026 for about two-thirds of its price new. Collector premiums tend to go to the V8 cars, special editions and cars with a story. This car has two of the three. Nobody can promise appreciation. A well-documented history is what gives it the best chance.
