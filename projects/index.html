Kalah

A multi-tenant church website and management platform for South African Reformed congregations. Each congregation gets its own site and its own admin, bilingual in Afrikaans and English. It is run as a non-profit and is free for every congregation.

Prison ministry

I am a volunteer mentor with Crossroads Prison Ministries. Incarcerated students work through Bible-study courses by correspondence; each week I mark their lessons, assess the devotional, and write each student a personal letter. I am also translating the Great Truths of the Bible and Survey of the Bible curricula into Afrikaans.
